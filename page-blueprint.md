# RankReels Founders Sales Page Blueprint

## Strategy

Audience: busy founders first; coaches, consultants, marketers, creators, and educators second. Primary action: purchase the Founders’ Deal at `$297/year`.

Narrative: promise → format proof → attention mechanism → production bottleneck → solution → product → demonstration → control/features → audiences → offer/value → guarantee → FAQ → urgency.

All copy and placeholders come from `sales-copy.md` and must remain verbatim during implementation.

## Global frame

- Slim navigation with RankReels logo, minimal anchors, login, and primary CTA.
- Add a launch bar only when end data is confirmed.
- Consider a sticky mobile CTA after the checkout destination and offer state are confirmed.
- Reuse approved RankReels legal/support links and the existing results disclaimer in the footer.

## CTA cadence

Place the primary CTA in the hero, after the demo, at the first founders-offer section, after unit economics, inside the pricing card, and in the final close. Use one approved destination unless the user defines multiple funnel steps.

## Proof and compliance checkpoints

- Verify view counts and permission for creator proof.
- Confirm `$297/year`, `$708/year`, `$411` savings, 300-reel allowance, and `$0.99` calculation.
- Resolve “unlimited access” against annual/credit limits.
- Reconcile both guarantee descriptions.
- Confirm launch timestamp, timezone, limited-spots claim, and expiry behavior.
- Confirm product claims including “look and sound exactly like you,” daily output, and setup in under five minutes.
- Supply approved checkout/signup, privacy, terms, disclaimer, refund, and support destinations.
