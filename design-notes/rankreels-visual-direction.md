# RankReels visual direction notes

- Do not place raw screenshots and videos together as a literal overlay unless the composition explains the product story.
- Prefer clean SaaS-style diagrams: component cards, connector lines, cropped UI specimens, and visual flow from inputs to platform to output.
- Product reveal section should use the same Apple-style design language as the feature visuals: one main framed canvas, red/yellow/green dots, blurred RankReels dashboard screenshot as the background workspace, sharp recreated RankReels logo at the top-left, compact left-side Face/Voice/Idea/Brand cards with only icon + title, a small central RankReels hub/mark where the input lines meet, right-side finished reel as the visual hero, and faint dashed connector lines that show the inputs combining into the output. Avoid heavy explanatory labels inside the visual.
- Product reveal must not rely on missing favicon/image assets for the top-left logo or central hub. Use a sharp recreated RankReels mark paired with the logo text at the top-left and a small standalone mark in the hub. Keep the background dashboard softly blurred but still visibly recognizable as the dashboard, and leave the finished reel phone/player styling unchanged once approved.
- Proof section with vertical social screenshots should not force the phone evidence to fill a horizontal placeholder. Use a horizontal Apple-style proof board instead: keep vertical screenshots intact as overlapping phone/profile cards, then place the supporting caption/insight text as a clean proof card beside them.
- VSL and sample reel sections should use media extracted from rankreels.ai: the VSL can use the three looping RankReels MP4 clips stacked like vertical phones, and the sample section should use four strong reel visuals from the source gallery. Footer extraction from rankreels.ai is planned for later.
- VSL should not show a visible placeholder/frame around the stacked media; keep the VSL media itself as the visual and pull it closer to the hero copy. Sample reel cards should be smaller, numberless, and can show five items on desktop.
- Real RankReels screenshots should remain present, but they should be cropped, framed, and contextualized so the viewer understands the mechanism.
- Avoid bottom tabs, radio-button-like labels, or decorative controls that do not serve a clear story purpose.
- When using reel footage, consider cropped detail views such as a face tile or output specimen instead of always showing the full phone video.
- The graphic should communicate: face, voice, idea, brand, and platform systems come together to generate the finished ranking reel.
- Keep the look premium and product-led: dark surfaces, restrained borders, coral highlights, subtle depth, clean map lines, and enough whitespace.
- For workflow screenshots, use wide horizontal frames rather than tall/narrow cards.
- When a screenshot is supporting context, use it as a softly blurred background and bring only the important UI area forward as a sharp cutout.
- Avoid visible floating text labels around screenshots. If extra explanation is needed, prefer small icon-only cues or a focused crop of the actual UI.
- For final render/review screens that already look clean, do not add extra diagram elements; frame the screenshot well and let the product screen speak.
- Reference screenshots from other products are only for proportion and minimalism. Do not copy their visible labels, headers, recording indicators, logos, or UI language.
- Workflow frames may use a small RankReels brand label, but should otherwise let the real product screenshot and focused pop-out card carry the meaning.
- If the user provides exact cropped UI sections for a workflow step, use those crops directly as the raised/focused card. Do not infer a new crop from the full screenshot and risk cutting off the key explanatory UI.
- For feature-card screenshots, the product screen should cover the full placeholder/media space. Use a clean Mac-style window treatment with simple red/yellow/green dots when requested, then bring the exact supplied focus crop forward as the prominent readable layer.
- For the “Keep Every Video on Brand” feature, follow the user’s generated layout direction but rebuild it inside RankReels styling: Apple-style window frame, dark warm background, two left input cards, three right output images, and faint dashed coral connector lines. Do not use the gray generated-image background. Keep the “Brand info” and “Writing guide” labels light, not bold.
- For the “Keep Every Video on Brand” connector specifically, use a literal map: the two left lines must touch the right edges of the “Brand info” and “Writing guide” cards, merge into one center line, then branch vertically on the right and touch the left edges of all three photos. Add small dot nodes at each connection point.
- For the “Your Face, Your Voice” identity section, use the existing vertical placeholder intentionally: preview portrait on top, a visual connector, and a compact editor timeline below with photo thumbnail, voice waveform, and brand/style blocks.
- For the identity visual, avoid nested frame-on-frame compositions. Use one main vertical surface that fills the placeholder. The timeline should read like a real editor: ruler ticks, track lanes, image thumbnail layer, obvious audio waveform layer, brand/style layer, and visible connector branches up to the preview.
- For the identity visual, keep the off-white/gray grid only in the upper preview area. The lower editor timeline should use the darker warm RankReels surface so the timeline lanes, waveform, and brand blocks remain legible.
- Updated identity direction: do not use an app/window placeholder for the digital twin section. The visual should be a horizontal concept card below the copy on the dark blue section background, with a thicker tasteful blue/cyan border. Inside, show five scenes: one large hero image on the left and four smaller cards in a 2x2 grid on the right, all with the same face but different clothing, scenes, and short ranking topic titles. The story is: one AI version of you can be reused for every reel that follows.
- Identity scene refinement: scene 1 is the uploaded source photo and should not have a title overlay. It should keep a natural portrait proportion, not feel squeezed. A faint warm dashed connector should run from the source photo to the four generated reel scenes on the right. All five photos should have warm yellow/orange borders, while scene titles should only appear on scenes 2-5 as lighter-weight centered text on compact black highlight chips.
